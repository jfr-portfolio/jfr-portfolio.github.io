## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an empathetic, luminous approach to mental health, psychological counseling, and mindful well-being. Departing from sterile clinical interfaces and generic corporate wellness apps, it marries the narrative depth of fine editorial design with the expressive, human-centric warmth found in contemporary illustration.

### Emotional Resonance & Persona
- **Empathetic & Anchoring:** Evokes safety, calm validation, and emotional stability through grounded cobalt blues and soothing linen backdrops.
- **Vibrant & Life-Affirming:** Introduces expressive, optimistic terracotta, sunlit ochre, and gentle lilac tones that inspire agency, growth, and optimism.
- **Intimate Editorial Craft:** Feels like stepping into a curated literary salon or private studio retreat, balancing spacious quietude with tactile richness.

### Design Movement: Modern Illustrated Editorial
The visual signature combines high-contrast literary typography, organic surface layers, and tactile, pillowed containers. Visual layouts are informed by rich storytelling, rhythmic white space, and soft boundary structures that alleviate sensory overwhelm while retaining unmistakable modern flair.

## Colors

The color palette grounds contemplative therapeutic work in warm light and balanced chromatic accents:

- **Primary (`#1D2D50` - Deep Cobalt Ink):** Serves as the anchor for primary actions, authoritative headings, and secure framing. It establishes trust, introspection, and depth without the severe coldness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#E76F51` - Radiant Terracotta):** Infuses warmth, human presence, and active guidance. Used for primary CTAs, key interactive highlights, and vital milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#E9C46A` - Golden Ochre):** Radiates optimism and gentle enlightenment. Reserved for badges, focus moments, warm tags, and celebratory affirmations.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Linen / Ivory):** The baseline canvas. It breathes natural tactility into screens, avoiding optical strain and clinical sterility.
- **Accents (Sage `#A8BCA1` & Lilac `#D8CFE6`):** Soft restorative pigments for secondary tags, gentle category categorization, reflective callouts, and organic card backgrounds.

Color application must prioritize high legibility, pairing `#1D2D50` or `#131C31` over linen and pastel tints to maintain WCAG AAA visual access standards.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances literary sophistication with effortless readability:

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Infuses organic warmth, transitional editorial elegance, and human craftsmanship. Its expressive letterforms elevate therapeutic quotes, section headliners, and clinical introductions into welcoming spaces.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & UI Elements):** Provides an open, friendly, geometric yet humanist counterweight. High x-heights and gentle apertures guarantee superior optical clarity for booking schedules, session narratives, clinical forms, and reflective readings across all screen sizes.

Headlines larger than 32px adapt fluidly to their corresponding mobile scales (`display-lg-mobile`, `headline-lg-mobile`), safeguarding screen rhythm and avoiding awkward line wraps on handheld viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout architecture prioritizes generous, calming spatial rhythm to prevent emotional and cognitive overload:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`container-max: 1200px`) centered with `margin-desktop` (64px) and `gutter-desktop` (32px). Consolidates to a 6-column structure on tablet devices and a 4-column structure on mobile devices with `margin-mobile` (20px) and `gutter-mobile` (16px).
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Staggering:** Select cards and narrative intros break strict symmetric blocks, featuring subtle offset margins (e.g., 24px vertical translations) that evoke literary magazine compositions.
- **Restorative Whitespace:** Section transitions employ comfortable padding scales (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) ensuring each area stands distinct, inviting deliberate breathing space between content clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews artificial skeuomorphism in favor of soft, atmospheric depth reminiscent of natural ambient light:

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily established through stacked surfaces. Over the base linen canvas (`#FAF7F2`), cards and panels sit on subtle cream tones (`#F2EDE4`) or tinted translucent pastels (Lilac and Sage washes).
- **Pillow Shadows:** Floating elements (such as active booking dialogues, elevated service cards, and sticky consultation triggers) utilize wide-radius, ultra-soft tinted drop shadows:
  - *Resting:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(29, 45, 80, 0.05)`
  - *Hover / Float:* `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(29, 45, 80, 0.09)`
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Interactive surfaces and nested containers utilize whisper-soft borders (`1px solid rgba(29, 45, 80, 0.06)`) that gently frame elements without disrupting the organic visual flow.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes softness, safety, and human approachability:

- **Primary Geometry:** Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, notification badges) feature comfortable 16px to 24px radii (`rounded-lg` and `rounded-xl`).
- **Feature Cards & Modals:** Key containers apply generous corner treatment (20px to 24px), eliminating sharp angles that create subconscious visual tension.
- **Pill Badges:** Small categorizations, availability tags, and micro-filters use full pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) to communicate warmth and tactile friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid terracotta (`#E76F51`) background with pure white text, bold rounded corners (16px), and generous horizontal padding (24px). On hover, gently transitions to deep cobalt (`#1D2D50`) with an ambient lift shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Deep cobalt (`#1D2D50`) outline or filled canvas with ivory text, paired alongside terracotta for high-priority dual flows (e.g., "Schedule Consultation" next to "Explore Approach").
- **Ghost / Text Button:** Deep cobalt text with an underline accent rendered in ochre (`#E9C46A`) or terracotta, transitioning smoothly on hover with a 2px vertical drift.

### Cards & Editorial Containers
- **Therapist / Service Cards:** Built on warm off-white surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F2EDE4`) framed by a 1px ghost border (`rgba(29, 45, 80, 0.06)`) and 20px rounded corners. Content pairs a Playfair headline with Plus Jakarta Sans body copy, accompanied by warm illustrated iconography or portrait framing with soft sage/terracotta backdrop backings.
- **Reflection / Testimonial Cards:** Tinted in muted sage (`#A8BCA1` at 15% opacity) or soft lilac, utilizing a large stylized quotation glyph in ochre and Playfair Display italic body text.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Specialty Chips:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 32px height, styled in soft neutral linen with `#1D2D50` text.
- **Active State:** Inverts into solid cobalt blue with crisp white text, or warm terracotta with a subtle border, providing clear sensory feedback during specialty filtering (e.g., "Anxiety", "Life Transitions", "Couples Therapy").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Generous 48px height with 12px rounded borders, subtle warm-linen fill (`#F2EDE4`), and deep cobalt typography. 
- **Focus State:** 2px solid terracotta (`#E76F51`) border with a delicate terracotta aura (`rgba(231, 111, 81, 0.15)` ring), removing default browser focus styles for an intentional, supportive feeling.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes & Radio Buttons:** Rounded profiles (6px for checkboxes, circular for radios) with 2px stroke in `#1D2D50`. When checked, surfaces fill with terracotta and display a crisp white checkmark/dot.

### Booking & Availability Bar
- **Consultation Bar:** A sticky or docked bottom banner on mobile/desktop crafted in deep cobalt with warm linen text, an ochre status indicator ("3 spaces available this week"), and a radiant terracotta CTA.
