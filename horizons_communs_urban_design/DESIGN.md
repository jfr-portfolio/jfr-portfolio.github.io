## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, warm, and participatory approach to urban design, civic activation, and social third-places. The emotional resonance blends the tactile craft of screenprinting and risograph workshops with the authoritative rigor of high-profile contemporary architectural monographs.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Municipal partners & civic innovators:** Seeking credibility, ecological responsibility, and clear community activation metrics.
- **Neighborhood collectives & everyday citizens:** Welcomed by non-intimidating, vibrant, humanistic touchpoints that encourage democratic placemaking.
- **Architects & cultural curators:** Captivated by high-craft editorial sensibility, architectural rhythm, and bold color layering.

### Design Movement: Risograph Editorial & Mediterranean Modernism
The visual language draws inspiration from modern editorial illustration (Alice Lee, Marly Gallardo, Maya Ealey) merged with Mediterranean architectural warmth:
- **Ink Overprint & Pigment Vibrancy:** Rich spot colors layered over an unbleached tactile warm paper substrate (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Structured Architectural Geometry:** Clear grid discipline, rhythmic hairline architectural separators, and considered negative space balancing high-chroma vibrancy.
- **Human Warmth:** Organic soft curves balancing grounded geometric structures, avoiding sterile corporate tech tropes.

## Colors

The palette simulates pure risograph ink drums on heavyweight warm cotton stock. Rather than standard digital neutrals, every shade is warm-tinted and earthy.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary — Terracotta Riso (`#C84B31`):** Sunbaked brick, clay tiles, raw earth. Primary actions, key framing accents, and high-impact structural focal points.
- **Secondary — Cobalt Horizon (`#1E429F`):** Deep Mediterranean sky, maritime depth, clarity. Used for interactive links, informational tags, and structural contrasts.
- **Tertiary — Ochre Sun (`#D97706`):** Vibrant noon light, civic energy. Used for celebratory accents, secondary badges, workshop tags, and highlights.
- **Quaternary Accent — Sage Canopy (`#3E6B56`):** Urban forestry, public parks, sustainable infrastructure. Used for ecological indicators, status positive badges, and landscape overlays.
- **Surface / Background — Paper Blanc (`#FAF8F5`):** Unbleached newsprint/cotton paper. Warm, non-glare canvas that makes spot colors sing.
- **Neutral / Text — Carbon Ink (`#23211E`):** Deep charcoal brown-black, avoiding pure `#000000` to maintain the tactile printed ink appearance.

### Overprint & Interaction Rules
Interactive hover states use slight ink bleed simulation: background fills shift into 8-12% tints of the primary/secondary inks, preserving the paper texture beneath.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs literary, expressive serif editorial styling with pragmatic architectural sans and technical label forms:

- **Headline Font (`Newsreader`):** Infuses civic manifestos, project monograph titles, and space descriptions with literary gravitas, warmth, and artistic heritage. It handles italics with dramatic humanistic flair.
- **Body Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm geometric geometry with subtle humanist touches that make public documentation, community notices, and long-form research inviting and effortless to parse.
- **Label & Annotation Font (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a crisp, technical architectural counterweight for coordinate data, plot numbers, dates, scale markers, and UI buttons.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured architectural rhythm: generous exterior margins framing content like an open publication layout.

### Grid Philosophy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `4rem` outer boundary margins and `2rem` gutters. Content frequently spans `7:5` or `8:4` asymmetric proportions to create editorial dynamism rather than predictable corporate halves.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Cards and architectural case studies reflow into 2-column paired modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column media stacks vertically, maintaining prominent hero framing.

### Section Density & Editorial White Space
Vertical pacing alternates between dense information blocks (e.g., project factsheets, participatory schedules) and expansive open pauses (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) that let high-chroma riso artwork and editorial photography breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately avoids generic blurred drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal pigment layering**, **crisp hairline containment**, and **tangible physical offset planes**:

- **Hairline Architectural Framing:** Surfaces are delineated by crisp, low-contrast ink borders (`1.5px solid #23211E` or `1px solid rgba(35, 33, 30, 0.15)`).
- **Physical Offset Blocks (Hard Print Shadows):** Floating interactive elements, highlighted event notices, and sticky modules use hard, unblurred offset dropouts: `4px 4px 0px #23211E` or `4px 4px 0px #C84B31`. This reinforces the tactile print aesthetic.
- **Color Overlay & Multiply Depth:** Layered image cards and color blocks simulate the ink overprinting of physical risograph machinery by using CSS `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on spot color washes against `#FAF8F5`.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses architectural craft: refined, softly chamfered, and grounded.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards):** Built with `roundedness: 1` (4px / 0.25rem border-radius). This delivers subtle softness without diluting the crisp, structural lines of urbanist plans.
- **Framed Imagery & Vignettes:** Occasionally feature distinctive arched tops (`border-radius: 4rem 4rem 4px 4px`) evoking porticos, arches, and Mediterranean civic structures.
- **Tags & Badges:** Keep the sharp 4px or pill-corner contrast to signify metadata status cleanly.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Terracotta Riso (`#C84B31`) background with warm cream text (`#FAF8F5`), 1.5px border in `#23211E`, and a `3px 3px 0px #23211E` hard offset shadow. On hover, the offset collapses to `1px 1px` with a subtle translate effect. Typography is uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`label-caps`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with warm cream fill, `1.5px solid #23211E` border, Carbon Ink text. Hover shifts background to a 10% tint of Ochre Sun (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Tertiary Action / Ghost:** Underlined editorial text link with an arrow glyph (`→`) that expands tracking on hover.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Urban Project Card:** Set on warm parchment (`#FFFFFF` or `#F4F0EA`), framed by a 1px border (`rgba(35, 33, 30, 0.12)`). Images feature a dual-tone or warm grain overlay. Metadata (neighborhood, typologies, year) sits in a top header strip using `Space Grotesk`.
- **Event / Gathering Card:** Background washed with pale Ochre or Sage tint, containing headline serif title, date stamp pill, and participant counter.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** High-contrast, compact capsules featuring `label-caps` in `Space Grotesk`. 
  - Civic Workshop: Ochre Sun tint (`#FDE68A`) with `#78350F` text.
  - Public Space / Park: Sage Canopy tint (`#D1FAE5`) with `#065F46` text.
  - Ongoing Consultation: Terracotta tint (`#FEE2E2`) with `#991B1B` text.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Input Fields:** Generous padding (`0.875rem 1rem`), warm cream fill (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5px solid rgba(35, 33, 30, 0.25)` border, and sharp focus rings rendered in solid Cobalt Horizon (`#1E429F`) with zero blur. Placeholder text is rendered in muted warm ink (`rgba(35, 33, 30, 0.45)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom tactile squares (checkboxes) and diamonds (radios) framed in `2px solid #23211E`. Selected states fill with terracotta ink and an organic custom check icon.

### Participatory Placemaking Widgets (Custom Components)
- **Consultation Progress Meter:** Architectural block progress bar filled with alternating diagonal riso stripes rather than smooth generic gradients.
- **Studio Manifesto Pull-Quote:** Spanning full columns, featuring oversized Newsreader italic serif quotes flanked by vertical terracotta ink ruler lines.
