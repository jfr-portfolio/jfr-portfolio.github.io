## Brand & Style

This design system establishes the visual voice for a progressive, activist legal collective championing social justice, labor rights, and women's advocacy. Moving far away from sterile, corporate legal tropes—mahogany panels, gilded scales of justice, and muted navy conservatism—the design embodies **High-Contrast Editorial Activism** infused with the expressive energy of modern illustrative arts (evocative of Alice Lee, Marly Gallardo, and Maya Ealey).

### Audience & Mood
The platform engages union organizers, individuals navigating systemic discrimination, whistleblowers, and modern cultural institutions seeking righteous legal counsel. The experience must evoke:
- **Radical Empathy & Solidarity:** Approachable, humane, and deeply authentic.
- **Unflinching Rigor:** Formidable legal command articulated through bold typographical contrast and structured rhythm.
- **Optimistic Defiance:** Vibrant, intentional chromatic power over cold institutional detachment.

### Visual Language
The aesthetic marries literary, high-contrast serif headlines with a contemporary humanist geometric sans-serif for UI density. Layouts rely on rhythmic, poster-like grid breaks, tactile paper-like backdrops, bold chromatic accent stripes, and custom-illustrated framing devices that transform standard legal notices and case studies into powerful rallying cries.

## Colors

The palette rejects clinical grays in favor of an organic, ink-on-parchment atmosphere punctuated by high-voltage activist pigments.

### Functional Roles & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#1a2d6b` - Electric Cobalt):** Serves as the bedrock of legal authority, primary typography, deep header banners, and high-impact structural borders. It conveys deep intellect and decisive protection.
- **Secondary (`#ff5a36` - Terracotta Coral):** The activist catalyst. Used for critical interactive triggers, call-to-action buttons, high-priority notifications, and fiery editorial pullouts.
- **Tertiary (`#f8b832` - Sunflower Amber):** Warmth, optimism, and illumination. Utilized for secondary highlights, badge backdrops, tag pills, and warning indicators that demand thoughtful attention without alarmism.
- **Support Forest (`#194d33` - Deep Emerald):** Reserved for legal confirmations, progressive case victories, verified statuses, and environmental/labor equity badges.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#faf7f2` - Warm Parchment):** The tactile ground plane. Replaces stark `#ffffff` with a warm, editorial eggshell that softens high chromatic contrasts and reduces eye strain across long-form legal briefings.
- **Surface Elevation (`#ffffff`):** Restricted to foreground cards, modal dialogs, and input containers to lift content crisply off the parchment ground.
- **Ink Secondary (`#4a5568`):** Muted slate for secondary metadata, timestamps, and supportive legal disclaimers.

## Typography

The typographical tension between **Playfair Display** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** orchestrates the firm's balance between legal gravitas and community accessibility.

- **Playfair Display** introduces an unapologetic, editorial elegance. With dramatic stroke transitions and sweeping serifs, it gives voice to manifesto declarations, victim testimonials, case outcomes, and section titling. It demands attention and commands moral weight.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** grounds the system with geometric clarity, spacious counters, and friendly openness. It maintains high legibility across dense statutory citations, client questionnaires, intake workflows, and UI controls.

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
1. **Case Openers:** Lead paragraphs under hero headings take `quote-editorial` or `body-lg`, set in Primary Cobalt or muted slate, introducing cases with journalistic cadence.
2. **Uppercase Labeling:** Category tags and procedural status pills (`label-md` and `label-lg`) are rendered in uppercase tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) to establish quick scanning markers against editorial serif banners.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid anchored by an editorial layout philosophy. The rhythm shifts purposefully between expansive, magazine-style features and tight, functional operational blocks.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and variable margins up to a max width of 1280px. Layouts alternate between full-width content blocks (8–10 columns) and staggered, multi-column dossiers (e.g., a 7-column case narrative paired with a 5-column action manifest).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Two-up card grids collapse into single or asymmetric dual pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and margins. Asymmetric art blocks stack beneath bold headline typography.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical whitespace is generous (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl` between major advocacy domains) to foster a deliberate, unhurried reading experience reminiscent of an activist journal or broadsheet. Component internals adhere to a disciplined 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` for compact badges, `space-xl` for card padding).

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid generic software gloss, this design system treats elevation as **tactile print layering and low-contrast physical displacement** rather than digital drop-shadows.

### Elevation Strategies
1. **Tonal Planar Stacking:** Surfaces step forward via color and contrast rather than heavy blur. Crisp, white cards (`#ffffff`) rest atop the warm parchment canvas (`#faf7f2`), framed by 1px to 1.5px solid borders in `#1a2d6b` (at 12% opacity) or tinted parchment contours (`#e8e2d5`).
2. **Illustrated Color Shadows ("Hard Offsets"):** For high-energy interactive components, primary CTA buttons, and highlighted legal dossiers, depth is achieved using crisp 3px or 4px solid offset shadows (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #1a2d6b`). This technique directly honors the bold graphic novel and screen-printing influences of the referenced artwork.
3. **Ghost Inset Borders:** Modals and focal drawers utilize a subtle 1px inner keyline in sunflower amber or electric cobalt to mimic fine bookbinding and letterpress borders.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects an intentional synthesis between structured legal order and human warmth. 

- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and modal envelopes adopt **Level 2 (Rounded)** corners (8px base, 16px for large thematic containers), eliminating harsh bureaucratic rigidity while preserving structural authority.
- Filter chips, metadata badges, and secondary action pills break into full pill-shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`), referencing political campaign buttons, activist pins, and editorial sticker callouts.
- Highlighting brackets, quotation shields, and illustrated frames occasionally employ asymmetric corner radii (e.g., `16px 0px 16px 0px`) on editorial teasers to evoke artisanal poster cuts and screen-printed flyers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Activist CTA:** Rendered in Terracotta Coral (`#ff5a36`) with white text, `font-weight: 700`, uppercase letter-spaced labels, rounded-lg (8px) corners, and a solid 3px electric cobalt hard offset (`box-shadow: 3px 3px 0 #1a2d6b`). On active click, translates 2px diagonally to produce a physical letterpress press.
- **Secondary Solidarity Button:** Deep Electric Cobalt (`#1a2d6b`) background with crisp white typography for institutional pathways (e.g., "Consulter la jurisprudence").
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent ground, 1.5px border in Primary Cobalt, shifting to Sunflower Amber background fill on hover with smooth color transition.

### Cards & Legal Dossiers
- **Case File Card:** Crisp white container framed with a delicate border (`#1a2d6b` at 15%). Displays a top 4px thematic accent stripe corresponding to legal sector (e.g., Terracotta for Women's Rights, Sunflower Yellow for Labor & Collective Bargaining, Forest Emerald for Social Equity).
- **Testimonial & Quote Card:** Built on Sunflower Yellow (`#f8b832` at 12% tint) or soft Eggshell with an oversized Playfair Display quotation glyph rendered in Coral Terracotta.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), featuring high-contrast text paired with soft chromatic container fills (e.g., Forest Emerald background `#e8f2ec` with `#194d33` text for "Victoire Juridique"; Terracotta tint with `#ff5a36` for "Audience en cours").
- **Interactive Topic Chips:** Eggshell background, 1px cobalt border, turning full Cobalt with white text when selected.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text & Area Fields:** Set against clean white or pale warm parchment with a distinct 1.5px border in slate-tinted cobalt. Upon focus, activates a crisp Terracotta Coral outline without blurry halo glows. Field labels sit in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold directly above the field with generous input padding (14px 16px).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted 20px boxes featuring 4px rounded corners. Checked state triggers solid Electric Cobalt fill with a bold white checkmark.

### Specialized Legal Activist Components
- **Case Progress Milestones:** An editorial vertical timeline combining numbered sunflower badges with high-contrast serif headers detailing legal stages (Inquiry, Filing, Oral Arguments, Court Verdict).
- **Emergency Hotline / Whistleblower Banner:** Electric Cobalt container crowned with bold Coral typography, sunflower warning icons, and high-visibility encrypted communication badges.
